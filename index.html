Questions

Which plants work best?

Most houseplants, herbs and leafy greens do well: pothos, basil, peace lily, ferns and mint. Cacti and succulents prefer to dry out, so use the pot without filling the tank.

How often do I refill it?

Every 2 to 4 weeks for most plants. Thirsty plants in bright rooms use more.

Will it leak?

No. The tank is sealed, and an overflow port stops it from overfilling.

Can I return it?

Yes. Send it back within 30 days for a full refund.
